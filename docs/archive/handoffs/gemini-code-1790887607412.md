## 4. Technical Diagnostics & Discoveries

1. **DFS Channel Availability Check (CAC) Latency:**  
   Channel 100 is within the European 5 GHz DFS band (5500 MHz)[cite: 10, 11]. Every configuration change or reboot forces the AP to listen silently for radar for at least 60 seconds before transmitting beacons. Rescanning too quickly after hitting Apply results in a false "missing radio" observation.

2. **Omada Multi-Tab Commit Architecture:**  
   In the Omada hardware controller GUI, the 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz radio tabs possess independent "Apply" triggers[cite: 9, 10]. Applying settings on 2.4 GHz does not push pending modifications on the 5 GHz tab[cite: 9, 10].

3. **Switch Discovery Artifact (Uplink/Downlink):**  
   Omada reporting Kitchen AP as a downstream child of Gallery AP is an expected cosmetic limitation when APs connect through an unmanaged or third-party switch (Netgear)[cite: 31, 32]. Tagged VLAN traffic routes correctly through the Firewalla.

4. **Android MAC Randomization:**  
   The Xiaomi 13T displays in Omada as randomized hardware address `DE-D8-CB-1A-DA-DC` on IP `192.168.10.137`, distinct from other household hardware such as `Redmi-Note-14` (`192.168.10.225`)[cite: 30, 31, 32].
